Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

LoL Scouter is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not collect personal data from visitors or from any other user.

What it stores

To publish videos, the tool stores access tokens for the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and X accounts in the owner's private storage. It also keeps the owner's queued videos and captions until they are posted.

How it uses platform data

Data received from TikTok, YouTube and X is used only to sign in to the owner's own accounts and to publish the owner's own videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Deletion

The owner can remove access at any time by revoking the app in each platform's settings, which makes the stored tokens useless, and can delete the stored tokens and queued videos.
